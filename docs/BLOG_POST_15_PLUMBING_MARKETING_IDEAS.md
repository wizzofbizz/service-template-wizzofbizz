# 15 Plumbing Marketing Ideas to Get More Business in 2025

As a plumber in South Africa, you know that fixing leaks and installing geysers is only half the battle. The other half? **Getting customers to find you instead of your competitors.**

The good news is that most plumbing businesses still rely on outdated marketing tactics (or none at all). That means with just a few smart strategies, you can stand out and win more jobs.

In this guide, we'll cover 15 practical, affordable marketing ideas that actually work for South African plumbers. No fluff, just actionable tactics you can implement today.

---

## 1. Claim and Optimize Your Google Business Profile

**Why it matters:** When someone searches "plumber near me" or "emergency plumber Cape Town," Google Business Profile (GBP) listings appear first—before any website.

**How to do it:**
- Claim your free GBP listing at [google.com/business](https://google.com/business)
- Complete every section: business hours, service areas, phone number
- Add high-quality photos of your team, van, and completed work
- Select the right categories: "Plumber," "Emergency Plumber," "Geyser Installation"
- Post updates weekly (tips, completed jobs, special offers)

## 6. Implement a Customer Referral Program

**Why it matters:** Referred customers are 4x more likely to hire you and spend 25% more on average.

## 10. Use Vehicle Wraps for Mobile Advertising

**Why it matters:** Your van is a moving billboard. Every trip to the plumbing supplier or job site is free advertising.

**What to include:**
- Business name and logo (large and readable)
- Services offered (burst pipes, geysers, drains)
- Phone number and website
- "24/7 Emergency Service" if applicable
- Before/after photos (optional)

**ROI:** A vehicle wrap generates 30,000-70,000 daily impressions and lasts 3-5 years.

## 13. Run Targeted Google Ads for High-Value Services

**Why it matters:** Google Ads puts you at the top of search results instantly (while SEO takes months).

**Best keywords to target:**
- "Emergency plumber [your city]"
- "Geyser installation [your city]"
- "Burst pipe repair [your city]"

## 14. Share Expert Plumbing Tips on Social Media

**Why it matters:** Educational content positions you as the trusted expert, not just another plumber.

**Content ideas:**
- Video: "How to prevent burst pipes in winter"
- Infographic: "5 signs you need a new geyser"
- Post: "Why is my toilet running constantly?"
- Reel: "Behind the scenes of a complex pipe repair"

Post 2-3 times per week on Facebook and Instagram. Consistency beats perfection.

---

## 15. Offer Seasonal Promotions

**Why it matters:** Seasonal offers create urgency and fill your schedule during slow periods.

**Promotion ideas:**
- **Winter:** "Free geyser blanket with installation" (May-July)
- **Spring:** "20% off drain cleaning" (September-October)
- **Summer:** "Pre-summer geyser check-up special" (November)
- **Year-round:** "10% off for pensioners" or "Free quote Fridays"

Promote these on your website, Google Business Profile, and WhatsApp status updates.
