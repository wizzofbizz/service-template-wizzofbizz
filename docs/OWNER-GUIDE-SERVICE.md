# Your Website: Owner's Guide

This is your manual for running the site day to day. No technical knowledge
assumed. Skim the first section, then come back to the rest as you need it.

---

## Your daily five minutes

Sign in at **yourdomain.co.za/login** and check these three things:

1. **Today's bookings** — the Dashboard shows today's jobs first. Who's coming,
   when, and where.
2. **New quote requests** — anything under Quotes marked *AI Quote* or *Draft*
   is waiting on you. Check the price looks right, then send it.
3. **Money owed** — the Dashboard shows unpaid and overdue invoices.

That's the whole routine. Everything else is as-needed.

## Getting set up (do this once)

### 1. Change your password

**Settings → Account**. Do this first — you were given a temporary one.

### 2. Your business details

**Settings → Business**. Your name, phone, email and address appear on quotes,
invoices and in emails to customers. Worth getting exactly right.

### 3. Your working hours

**Settings → Calendar**. This decides which times customers can book.

- **Working hours** — your open hours for each day. Leave a day blank if
  you're closed.
- **Minimum notice** — how much warning you need. Set to 24 hours and nobody
  can book for this afternoon.
- **How far ahead** — how far into the future bookings are allowed. Two weeks
  is a sensible default.
- **Gap between jobs** — travel or setup time. The site automatically leaves
  this gap.

> If customers say they can't find a time, this is almost always why. A brand
> new site has no working hours set, so nothing is bookable.

### 4. Your services

**Services**. Each one needs:

- **Name** — what the customer sees
- **Price** — leave blank if it always varies
- **How long it takes** — this decides how much of your calendar it blocks

Delete the demo services once yours are in.

### 5. How customers book

**Settings → Booking**. Three choices:

- **Book straight away** — they pick a service and a time, and it's confirmed.
  Best if your prices and appointment lengths are fixed.
- **Quote first, then book** — they describe the job, you send a price, they
  accept, then they book. Best if the price depends on the work.
- **Quotes only** — they ask for a quote and you arrange timing yourself.

You can change this whenever you like; it takes effect immediately.

## Everyday tasks

### Someone wants a quote

New requests appear under **Quotes**. Open it and you'll see what the customer
asked for and a suggested price.

**The suggested price is a starting point, not a decision.** It's worked out
from the rules you set on each service. Change any line, then **Send**.

The customer gets an email with a link to view it, accept it, and book a time.

### Someone books an appointment

It appears under **Jobs** and on your **Calendar**. The customer gets a
confirmation automatically.

To move it, open the job and change the date or time. To cancel, open it and
mark it cancelled.

### Finishing a job

Open the job → **Mark as complete**. If you have invoicing switched on, you'll
be prompted to create the invoice.

### Getting paid

**Invoices** shows everything outstanding.

- **Send** emails the customer a link to view and pay
- **Overdue** ones are highlighted, and chased automatically
- When a customer pays online, the invoice updates by itself

If they pay by EFT or cash, open the invoice and mark it paid yourself.

### Your customers

**Contacts** is your customer list. Click anyone to see everything you've done
with them — quotes, jobs, invoices, messages.

Contacts are created automatically when someone books or asks for a quote. You
can also add them by hand.

## What the site does without you

These run automatically — you don't have to remember any of them:

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| 3 days after a quote goes out | Polite follow-up if they haven't replied |
| Before an appointment | Reminder to the customer |
| Deposit not yet paid | Reminder before the deadline |
| Invoice goes overdue | Payment reminder |
| Any booking or quote | Immediate confirmation |

You can adjust the timings in **Settings**.

## Editing your public website

**Website Editor** lets you change what visitors see: your headline, the
services you list, the "about us" text, photos, and your contact section.

Changes go live immediately. If something looks wrong, change it back — nothing
is permanent.

## Taking payments online

**Settings → Payments**.

Switch on **Accept online payments** and customers get a "Pay now" button on
their invoices. You can allow it for invoices, deposits, or both.

If you see a note saying PayFast isn't connected yet, your payment details
haven't been added to the site's configuration. Ask whoever set the site up —
you can still switch this on now, and it will start working once they do.

Turn it off any time to go back to EFT or cash only.

## WhatsApp messages

If WhatsApp is set up, customers who opt in get their confirmations and
reminders on WhatsApp as well as email.

**Settings → WhatsApp** shows whether it's connected. If it disconnects you'll
get an email — messages queue up in the meantime and send once you reconnect,
so nothing is lost.

## Common questions

**A customer says they can't book a time**
Check **Settings → Calendar**. Usually working hours aren't set, minimum notice
is too long, or that day is marked closed.

**A quote's price looks wrong**
Change it before sending — you always have the last word. If it's consistently
wrong, adjust the pricing rules on that service under **Services**.

**I need to block out a day**
Add a job for that time. The slot stops being offered to customers.

**A customer didn't get their email**
Check **Contacts** that their address is right. If several people report this,
contact whoever set up your site — the email service may need attention.

**I want to change my prices**
**Services** → edit the price. It applies to new quotes and bookings straight
away; existing ones keep the price they were given.

**Can I see what happened with a customer?**
**Contacts** → click their name. Everything is there in date order.

## Getting help

If something looks broken rather than confusing, note:

- what you were doing
- what you expected
- what happened instead

That's usually enough for whoever supports your site to sort it out quickly.
